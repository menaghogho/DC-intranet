About the Digital Champions

Welcome to the Digital Champions intranet. We're a team embedded across South Warwickshire, Rugby, Coventry and Warwickshire-North, helping practices get real value out of digital tools like NHS App, EMIS and Accurx — and make sense of the data behind every appointment. This is a space for us to share and collaborate with all practices across the ICB.

Meet the team See the Resource page
What we do

Hands-on support, not another portal to check.

Digital Champions work alongside reception, clinical and management teams — running training, fixing the small things that slow a clinic down, and making sense of the data practices are already collecting.

NHS App & Accurx

Patient-facing tools set up properly, so more of the practice's workload can move online without extra calls to reception.

EMIS Web training

General training sessions run by the team, covering the day-to-day EMIS workflows staff actually use. Click to book.

Understanding GPAD

Plain-English guidance on how appointment slots get recorded and reported nationally.

AI & digital tools

Curated resources on the newer AI tools appearing in general practice, and how to use them safely.

Partnership working

Four places, one team.

The Digital Champions cover every place across the ICB, working in partnership with each practice on what matters most to them locally.
